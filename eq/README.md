# EQ and Crossover Filters

This module develops the signal-processing foundations behind DJ EQ sections, isolators, and multiband processing.

## Topics

- **Filter types:** low-pass, high-pass, band-pass, band-stop, shelving, and peaking filters; their cutoff frequencies, slopes, and resonance.
- **Three-band EQ:** separate low, mid, and high controls for practical tonal shaping while cueing and blending tracks.
- **Isolators:** steep, often full-cut controls that emphasize creative removal and restoration of frequency bands.
- **Crossover filters:** complementary low/mid/high filterbanks that divide a signal for processing or recombination.
- **Phase coherence:** group delay, polarity, and crossover alignment; complementary bands must sum predictably to avoid dips, peaks, and comb filtering.

Use listening tests and spectrum plots to compare conventional EQ controls with reconstruction-safe crossover designs such as Linkwitz-Riley filters.
